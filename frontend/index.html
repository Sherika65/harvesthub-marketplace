About HarvestHub

HarvestHub is a farmer-to-customer marketplace designed to connect local farmers directly with customers. Farmers can list their fresh products, manage orders, and reach customers without unnecessary middlemen.

Customers can explore available products, add items to their cart, provide delivery details, choose a payment method, place orders, and share reviews about their purchases.
